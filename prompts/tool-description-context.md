Control logical pages and recover archived context. `begin` requires `title` and `objective`. `complete` and `block` require `summary`, observed `evidence` and `next`. Call lifecycle operations alone after inspecting prior results; the harness archives and compresses before further work. `block` preserves an unfinished objective for resume. `list` locates pages. `read` requires `page` and supports `kind` (summary or transcript), line `offset` and `limit`. `search` requires a literal `query` and supports `page` and `limit`.
