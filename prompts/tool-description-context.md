Compact completed logical blocks into archived pages and recover archived context. Call `compact` (or `complete`) with `summary`, observed `evidence` and `next` (plus optional `title`, `findings`, `openRisks`, `result`, or `finalResult`) after finishing and verifying a logical block; the harness archives the transcript to disk and replaces active history with a compact summary before further work. `list` locates archived pages. `read` requires `page` and supports `kind` (summary or transcript), line `offset` and `limit`. `search` requires a literal `query` and supports `page` and `limit`.

A checkpoint preserves substantive findings, exact quotations and source identifiers in `findings`, the current partial deliverable in `result`, and unresolved issues in `openRisks`. When the entire task is finished, supplying `finalResult` saves the archive and publishes the final result atomically without another model request.
