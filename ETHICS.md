# Ethics

Flecten is free software under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The license grants you the legal freedom to use, study, modify, and share the software, including commercially. This page describes the ethical norms the community asks of everyone who participates. They are community expectations, not extra legal restrictions: the license terms alone govern the software.

## Freedom

Use Flecten for any lawful purpose, change it, and share your changes. Respect the same freedom in others: keep the license and notices intact, and give back improvements as the AGPL requires.

## Respect

Treat every contributor and user with dignity. No harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks. Critique the work, not the person.

## Understanding

Assume good faith. Communicate clearly and patiently, welcome newcomers, and respect different languages, contexts, and skill levels. Disagreement is welcome when it is grounded in facts.

## Do no harm

Do not use Flecten to harm, deceive, or violate the rights of others: no non-consensual surveillance, no abuse, no malicious deception. You are responsible for how you deploy and use it.

## Honesty

Do not misrepresent the project, its authors, or its outputs. Credit the people whose work you build on.

These norms guide participation in the project. See the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) for how they are upheld.
