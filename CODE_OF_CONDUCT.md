## Our pledge

We want Flecten to be a place where people are free to build and learn, and treat each other with respect and understanding. Participation is open to everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience.

## Standards

- Discuss code and decisions on the merits, with facts.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith; be patient and clear, especially with newcomers.
- Respect different languages, contexts, and viewpoints.
- Give and keep credit for others' work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Insults, harassment, bullying, threats, sexualized attention, discrimination, and publishing personal data without consent are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report violations privately to the repository owner. The owner may warn a participant, remove content, or restrict access. Reports are handled without public discussion.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces and to representing the project in public. It complements the ethical norms in [ETHICS.md](ETHICS.md); the software itself is governed by the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE).
